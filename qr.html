Sign sheet

Ten signs, one per variety. Set the address the codes should point at, then print.

Whatever you print here is permanent — the signs are laminated and staked out in a field. Get this right before printing, not after.

Before these go out

Matte laminate, never gloss. A phone torch on gloss makes a hotspot the camera cannot read through, and two of your October dates run to 6pm with the last week closing at dusk. This is the most common way a QR trail fails, and it is invisible until it is dark.

Mount at chest height where a torch reaches, and keep the white border around the code — trimming it stops the code scanning.

Staff key

Which sign is which. Keep this at the gate — it is not printed with the signs, because the signs must give nothing away.
